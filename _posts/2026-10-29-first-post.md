A notebook starts with a little space and a question worth coming back to. This is mine.

Life sciences, investing, and entrepreneurship are the starting points. The connections between them are often where the questions become most interesting. How does an idea become a useful product? What makes a business worth understanding? Which assumptions deserve a closer look?

This will be a place for essays, observations, and questions that deserve more than a passing thought. Some notes will be about businesses. Others will follow the science, or explore what it means to build something.

The aim is simple: make a little more sense of things, one note at a time. Put an idea into words. Notice what is missing. Come back with a better question.

For now, this is the first page. There is plenty of room for what comes next.
